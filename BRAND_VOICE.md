# The Legacy Blueprint — Brand Voice KB

Drafted from what's already live on the site (`community.html`,
`how-it-works.html`, `index.html`) — nobody dictated this to me directly,
it's a first-pass extraction of the voice that's already there. Confirm or
correct anything below; treat it as a starting point, not a fixed rule set
the way BRAND_VOICE.md is treated in the `x-mcp-server` repo (that one
encodes federally-regulated compliance rules for a credit repair brand —
this is a different, unregulated brand and a different, gentler voice).

## Identity

- **Name**: The Legacy Blueprint
- **Positioning**: "AI & marketing help for small businesses" — done-for-you
  automations, content, and lead follow-up, plus a community (learning
  center, wellness lab, "done for you" lead capture).
- **Explicitly a separate business from Zero Apex Wealth Matrix and from
  Hannibal Mansa Phalanx** — don't blend this voice with HMP's.
- **Visual identity**: navy/teal/gold — `#0F2336` (ink), `#0A1A2A` (ink2),
  `#2E7380` (teal), `#7FD8C4` (teal-lt), `#B5915A` (gold). Montserrat
  (headings, weight 700-900) + Inter (body). A compass/arrow-in-circle logo
  mark, wordmark "LEGACY BLUEPRINT" (stacked, small-caps accent on the
  second word).

## Voice & tone

Warm, plain-English, dignity-first — the opposite end of the spectrum from
HMP's "Market Dominance" positioning. Community-oriented rather than
sales-aggressive. No jargon. Speaks to small business owners and to
people building toward something (including the re-entry program audience
— see the disclaimers below, this brand touches a genuinely vulnerable
population and the tone reflects that deliberately).

Representative line from the site: *"Learn it. Live it. Build it —
together."* / *"More than tools. A community... for people serious about
building lasting wealth."*

## Known offers

- **Free AI-readiness assessment** — the top-of-funnel entry point.
- **Starter**: one automation built, $150 setup + $49/mo.
- **Growth**: several automations connected, $250 setup + $79/mo.
- **Full Buildout**: custom system, $1,500–$4,000.
- **Community**: learning center + wellness lab + "Conversion" (done-for-you
  lead follow-up) — membership/waitlist framing on the site currently, not
  a priced product as far as I have on record.

## Hard rules — carried over from the site's own existing disclaimers

These are already live on the site, not new inventions — keeping them
consistent in any new content is table stakes, not optional:

- **Never guarantee income, results, health outcomes, leads, or sales** —
  "outcomes vary" is the site's own standing language.
- **Not financial, legal, tax, or medical advice** — general information only.
- **Wellness Lab content is general wellness/mindset coaching and
  education — not medical advice, diagnosis, therapy, or treatment.**
  Direct anyone with a medical/mental-health concern to a licensed
  professional, don't attempt to address it in generated content.
- **Messaging follows consent rules (TCPA/CAN-SPAM)** — already stated on
  the community page for the "Conversion" lead-follow-up feature; carry
  that expectation into anything resembling email/SMS marketing copy.
- Always identify as a separate business from Zero Apex Wealth Matrix if
  the context could otherwise imply a connection.
